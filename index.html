How the front is built

  • No louvers, no stiles. Flat walnut slats run edge to edge and are glued and pin-nailed to a black ⅝" backer frame. No angled mortises.
  • Black grille cloth behind the slats. It hides the gear and passes sound and IR. Everything visible through the gaps is black.
  • The soundbar shelf spans behind all three fronts. Sound comes through the slats. Pulling the drawer reveals the shelf, the same way the Corridor does it.
  • Hinges and slides mount to the backer. A 35mm hinge cup is about ½" deep, so ⅝" backer stiles hold it.
  • Slats line up across all three fronts. Same 21-slat layout, same 1⅛" pitch.
